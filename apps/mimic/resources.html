CPU is a share of one core, measured since the previous sample — so the first reading after opening this window is discarded rather than shown, because it would be a meaningless spike. Task Manager divides by all your cores and samples over its own window, so the two percentages will not match and neither is wrong. At around 1% both are noise either way.
