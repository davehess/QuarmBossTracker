# Sound credits

The eight original sounds (alarm, bell, chime, gong, horn, pop, slap, whoosh) were generated with ffmpeg for this project.

The four weave sounds (added 2026-11-09) come from Freesound. They were trimmed to under a second, made mono and levelled
to the rest of the set (peak about −10 dB) with ffmpeg.
