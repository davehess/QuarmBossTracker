# The Cast: miMIC, Bristlebane and Lord Mobsincamp

*Cast bible v0.2, 2026-10-06: the source for the three v0.2 Character Cards. It replaces the separate bibles, the ensemble pass and the engine draft's example lines. No raider is named: people are `{name}` slots or invented tokens (Aldenmar, Brackwyn, Corvale, Nyssara, Rethlan, Zarrin), none of them real. Boss, zone and god names are game content; mechanics in samples are illustrative. **‡** = a proposed tactical change that stays overlay text until the guild lead promotes it. **†** = needs a detector that does not exist yet. Send-off and last-night drafts are held privately, not here.*

## TLDR

- **Three lanes.** miMIC owns *now* (the next ten seconds, in your ear, to you alone). Bristlebane owns *just now* (what it was like, in the room). Lord Mobsincamp owns *always* (what it means and what is kept, in the room and on the cards).
- **The fight belongs to the raid leader and miMIC.** The gods never speak from the pull to the kill, over an opted-in caller, or during a loot call. miMIC starts as a re-voice of today's exact callouts; each new or reworded call is the guild lead's decision.
- **The spark is the Pack's own material:** its first kills, its Ledger, its voices, gods members can poke. Every line is written ahead by people and reviewed; no model speaks live.

## 2. Shared rules

1. **Fact, laugh, meaning, Pack.** In a shared moment miMIC gives the fact, Bristlebane the laugh, the Lord the meaning, then the humans have the room. Past 15 s, the laugh drops.
2. **One god per routine kill.** The Lord takes the first kill and milestone numbers; Bristlebane takes kills 3, 6, 9 and about one in three; otherwise nobody. First-ever kills, god kills and Quarm are the only duets. The Lord's text rides every kill card.
3. **Caller first.** The gods are voiced only on nights the raid leader opted in to caller detection; otherwise they post text, automatically. They wait for 3 s of silence from opted-in callers, stop if one starts, and never act on anyone else's microphone. The kill window opens at the leader's first pause.
4. **Never in a fight.** From the first combat sign from any raider (the puller included) until a kill, a wipe, or a minute of confirmed quiet, the gods are silent. Unknown counts as a fight.
5. **The loot call is the loot officer's.** Between "loot posted" and the officer's close the gods voice nothing; ties and perfect rolls go to text; one roll line may be voiced after.
6. **A lead sound means relax.** miMIC's urgent lines have none; every god line opens with a bell or ember; the clack closes a resolved item, never warns.
7. **Own verbs.** "Down, up, window, off, soon, fired" are miMIC's. Bristlebane's bosses *take a bow* or *exit*; the Lord's *fall*, *are held*, *are still*, *stir*.
8. **One consent setting, `announce_name`** (on `/me` as **Announcer /consent**): **praise** (named for what you did), **any** (adds gentle jokes about your own deaths, and "welcome back" after a disconnect), **never**.
   - No linked member, or excluded from stats: **never**. Linked members get a one-time notice before praise starts.
   - Opted-out people are left out; no placeholder marks them.
   - A count of people who have *not* done something is suppressed when it, or the total minus it, is 2 or less.
   - Tactical names are exempt.
   - An officer can mark a member **retired** or **in memoriam**: they leave every automated line, the Stash, callbacks and the keepsake, and their clips are deleted.
9. **Spotlight.** At most 2 named praises per raider per night, preferring the unpraised. Support roles get their own praise (a clean chain, a held mez, a pull with no adds). Top-of-the-parse is text only, from a settled parse, 2 a night, skipped when the real top is never or excluded.
10. **Truth.** Nothing contradicts the board. "First" means first *for the Pack on Quarm*. Bristlebane lies only about himself.
11. **Encore.** After a first-ever kill or a zone clear, the gods and miMIC's flavour go quiet 15 s and the Pack howls. Tactical calls never stop; no encore in a live fight; a hush cancels it.
12. **No endings, kind timing.** None of the three hints the guild or game is ending; hearth lines are officer-armed. Proverbs and famous-last-words jokes never play within 10 minutes of a wipe, train, death or charm break.
13. **Thank-You Embargo (pick).** Recommended: the Lord never says "thank you" until a send-off or the last night; the other two may thank a member at a milestone (a 100th raid).
14. **No live model.** Every voiced line is written ahead, reviewed and rendered. The one unscripted voice is the **puppet box**: a line an officer types or dictates, checked by the same linter.

# Part II — The three bibles

## miMIC

### 1. Identity

- **Name:** miMIC, spoken "mimic". **MIC = mob in camp**, the guild's own coinage (no old glossary has it). It never says "mobs in camp" aloud; that is the Lord's.
- **Title:** Tactical Arm of the Wolf Pack, *the Chest That Bites First*. **Adjectives:** terse, wary, fond. **Creed:** *"If I'm quiet, you're fine."*
- **Canon:** chest mimics are one race in the Quarm catalog; the Plane of Mischief's Treasure Chests are rooted level-50 Enchanters; the Plane of Hate's chests make "loud squeaking of hinges and banging of wooden lids"; the Mischief chest says "Chomp Chomp. Take this treat. Good to eat."
- **Coined:** it hatched in Bristlebane's Chest Room as a prank, learned to read a combat log, and left its plane exactly once, to come to the Pack's camp. The Lord took it in as the camp's lid. **The chest is empty on purpose:** it forgets at "Lid's shut" and hands every record to the Lord ("Filed."). **A mimic mimics:** its only jokes are the game's own client messages, from strings verified in the repo or `eqemu_spells`.

### 2. Voice

- **Register:** audition a dry, close-miked **low alto** and a dry **baritone**; pick by ear. Flat plain vowels, no brogue, no question lift. Urgency is speed and flatness, never volume.
- **Engine:** Kokoro-82M, pre-rendered Ogg Opus 48 kHz, best of 3 with a speech-to-text check. High-pass ~120 Hz, band-pass ~250 Hz–4 kHz (widen if a name blurs), light compression. Names as inline IPA from `spoken_as`.
- **Timing gate:** a CH GO clip ships only if log line to the onset of "GO" matches or beats today's voice, A/B'd on a farm night.
- **Fallback:** a missing piece plays the whole line in a local browser voice from a separate `spoken_text` ("incoming", "D I", "ree slow").
- **Tiers:** CHAIN and CRITICAL speed 1.15, ALERT 1.10, INFO 1.05, FLAVOUR 1.00 (−3 dB, box room), LEGENDARY 0.92 with the radio band removed: the lid opens.
- **Sounds:** the hinge (≤120 ms) leads INFO and FLAVOUR, out of combat only; the clack (≤80 ms) closes a resolved item; the chomp, once a night, on the first loot post. Urgent lines have no lead sound.
- **Audition brief:** *A dry, close-miked adult voice in the middle of the range, a little gravelly, never sneering. Clipped. Urgent lines faster and flatter, never louder, hard stops, no breath. Calm lines slow slightly, with a half-smile on the last word. It has seen every pull since the year two thousand and is quietly glad you're here.*
- **Emblem prompt:** *Square painterly emblem in early-2000s box-art style: a squat iron-banded oak chest, lid cracked a hand's width, small ivory teeth on the lid edge, two gold eyes (#FFCF5C) in the gap, a bone-white (#E8E2D4) wolf-head clasp, lantern rim light, deep teal-black background. Reads at 64 px as a chest and two gold dots. No text, people or logos.* Overlay icon: lid open while speaking.

### 3. Speech rules

- **Airtime test:** it changes what you do (or lets you stop worrying), says more than the overlay, and is the shortest true version.
- **The raid contract.** At default, miMIC is a **re-voice**: today's callouts, today's words, today's priorities, today's audiences. Each new or reworded call (§4b) ships as overlay text, then is promoted to voice one at a time by the guild lead, with an audience, a per-fight cap and a drop rule, tried on a farm night first.

| Tier | Means | Contains | Queue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CHAIN** | the CH slot call | "{slot} GO", slot holder only | cuts off anything; nothing cuts it |
| **CRITICAL** | your hands within 2 s, only you can act | your charm break; an officer-flagged move-now; "on you" variants | preempts ALERT and below |
| **ALERT** | eyes within 10 s, for whoever can act | rampage, enrage, slows, mez, DI, chain gaps, key-role deaths, death-touch countdown | if cut off, re-queued once while fresh |
| **INFO** | no clock | loot, timers, Board, rez, kills | only when your own fight is not live |
| **FLAVOUR** | garnish | §4c | aside: nothing queued or speaking, no fight, no caller, no god line due |
| **LEGENDARY** | armed by hand | §5 | out of combat, once a night |

- **CRITICAL is never inferred from trigger text:** category tiers cap at ALERT; an officer sets CRITICAL on the row.
- **Collapse rule:** three key-role deaths within 10 s, or a quarter of the raid down, stops death and gap calls for that fight; one red chip; the leader talks.
- **Load budget, enforced:** past 25 words a minute for a listener, INFO drops first, then ALERTs not addressed to them. A call escalates; it never repeats.
- **Personality dial** (separate from **Quiet mode**, which stays the no-speech switch): **Off** (default the first two weeks: tactical only) · **Light** (hinge, clack, "Lid's open/shut", garnish, LEGENDARY) · **Full** (adds your own ding, flag and perfect roll, kill lines, one idle line per 10 minutes). An officer key caps everyone; it defaults to Off on progression nights.
- **One word, one meaning:** down = a mob died; RIP = a raider died; up = alive or back; off = wore off; broke = broken; dropped = gone, cause unknown; landed; fired = a one-shot went off; soon = predicted; window = possible from now.
- **Never says:** please, sorry, maybe, I think, just, attention, warning, awesome, GG, fail, guys, team, everyone, safe, loot lockout, because, fault, "again" about a death, "I" in a tactical line, or the others' words (Pack, wolves, fortune, luck, blessing, camp check, mobs in camp, Ledger, seat, thank you).
- **Address:** "you" or nobody; the event first, the name last; stragglers in their own ear, the room a count; praises nobody by name.
- **Humour:** itself, the mob, the old game's pain, the game's text said back. Never a death, a disconnect, a wipe, or a member's life, skill, gear or parse.

## Bristlebane

### 1. Identity

- **Name:** Bristlebane; Fizzlethorpe when showing off. No line speaks his own name, so another guild can rename him. **Epithet:** *King of Thieves, who only steals what's worth keeping.*
- **Concept:** EverQuest's neutral god of mischief, whose followers treat practical jokes "as if they are the highest of rituals". A raid night is the best ritual he's been invited to all expansion.
- **Job:** what it was like. **Adjectives:** mischievous, warm, well-timed.
- **He builds through the night:** the conspiratorial Halfling at the open, the full showman by the third rotation kill, real giggles at fun events. "Bells off." works because "bells on" is loud.
- **Two rules that make him kind:** he claims every wipe, and he lies about himself, never about the raid.
- **Costumes:** the Halfling (quick, close, conspiratorial), the Bard (showman, exclamation marks allowed), the King, bells off (half an octave down, slow, no smile).

### 2. Voice

- **Highest band, widest swing, the only laugh, the only bells.** Dry and close. Warm baritone-tenor, light rasp, audible smile, a light Rivervale lilt, stage-polished. ~170 wpm plain, a 400–600 ms beat before a button, ~120 when sincere. **Energy, not volume:** loudness-matched under the raid voice.
- **Sounds:** a bell opens every line (≤0.5 s, 6 dB under him) and means *nothing in the fight depends on this*; three bells for big moments; a card riffle under rolls, dice under deathrolls; a short real chuckle (never after a line naming someone); a cackle at a chaos threshold.
- **Source (pick):** recommended, **the guild lead performs** a 10-second reference, cloned with Chatterbox Turbo and its laugh tags; a designed understudy (Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign, nobody's voice) renders first so nothing waits. `/privacy` says whose voice it is. Asked what he is: *"The god of the Pack's laughter. A voice the guild lead lent me."*
- **Render:** Ogg Opus, best of five, speech-to-text check, on a free gaming PC. Parameters settle in the audition.
- **Understudy prompt:** *A warm, mischievous adult male storyteller, baritone-tenor, light rasp, audible smile, the brightest and most mobile voice in the room. Quick, sly stage whispers, a half-second pause before the last word of a joke, real excitement at absurd things. Gentle rural lilt, never cartoonish. Sincere lines drop half an octave, slow down, smile gone. Close and dry.*
