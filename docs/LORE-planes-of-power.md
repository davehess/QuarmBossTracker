# Lore: the Planes of Power and the pantheon (for raid narratives)

*Built 2026-09-28 from [Lore of Norrath](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/), a fan archive of
EverQuest in-game text, official web copy and developer notes. Six Haiku agents combed about 60 of
its ~990 posts; every claim below was then checked against the source text, and the agents'
mistakes are listed at the end so the next pass does not repeat them.*

**The story in one sentence:** the gods imprisoned Zebuxoruk for teaching mortals to understand the
immortal realms, his followers spent ages opening portals to find him, and when mortals finally
fight through every plane, kill the gods' own champions and break the Plane of Time, the gods decide
mortals have become too strong and withdraw from Norrath, which starts the slide into the Rending,
the Shattering of Luclin and the world of EverQuest 2.

So the raids we start on October 1 are, in the lore, **the reason the gods go silent.**

## The arc, in order

1. **Creation.** The Nameless finds space and time, then makes four **Gods of Power** to give the
   chaos shape: **the Rathe** (earth, who splits into a council), **Fennin Ro** (fire), **Water** as
   three (Tarew Marr, E'Ci for ice, Povar for steam), and **Xegony** (air). They in turn make the
   **Gods of Influence** in threes: a force of darkness, one of light, one in the middle to keep the
   balance. Veeshan is the first god to notice Norrath and drops her dragons on Velious.
   [Creation of Norrath](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2011/06/30/creation-of-norrath/) · B
   - This matters for PoP: **the Tier 5 elemental planes are the realms of those first four powers.**
     An NPC calls them "the true Planes of Power". The raid's endgame is fighting creation itself.
2. **Rallos Zek's war on the Plane of Earth (the Elder Age).** An ogre general, Murdunk, invades the
   Rathe's plane at the urging of Rallos Zek's eldest son **Eriak**, and loses: the Rathe regenerate.
   Rallos then sends two mortal war heroes, **Tallon and Vallon**, who capture the council and
   execute one member on Norrath (the Rathe Mountains rise where it fell). The Rathe, helped by other
   gods, curse every child of Zek: the giants' homelands freeze into Everfrost and every follower of
   Zek is left a lesser being in mind and body. Rallos, scarred by the Rathe's wards, pulls Tallon and
   Vallon into his plane to save them, tears the essence of war out of Eriak (who never warned him of
   the wards) and gives it to them. Eriak now forges weapons in the depths of Drunder, and Rallos still
   carries the scars. [Invasion of Earth](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2011/06/06/invasion-of-earth/) · B
   - Mithaniel Marr had made the minotaurs to embody honour; Rallos lured them into that war and
     they were cursed with the rest. [Journal of a Disciple of Marr](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2013/06/27/journal-of-a-disciple-of-marr/) · A
3. **Zebuxoruk, the one who taught.** He was the first to give mortals a real grasp of the immortal
   realms. Those who understood rose in power, and those who misused it went mad. The Elder who tells
   this names **the Shissar** as the example, and says the Sebilisians and the Combine also followed
   his teachings. The pantheon took him prisoner for heresy. His followers, the "followers of
   balance", opened the first portals into the gods' realms, looking for him, and word of those
   portals is how other mortals got in.
   [About Zebuxoruk](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2010/07/14/about-zebuxoruk/) (Elder Aytael's dialogue) · A
4. **The planes open (the 2002 launch story).** Gray-robed figures quietly rob the great libraries of
   every city and leave no memory behind but "time was short". Karana, troubled, hands his servant
   **Askr** a great medallion and withdraws to meditate, and in his vision a white-bearded face
   swallows his mind: **Agnarr's**. In Drunder, Rallos Zek burns Tallon's face over a delay that Tallon
   blames on meddling mortals. Bertoxxulous swats away a message from Innoruuk about mortal meddling: "Nothing can change
   what has been done!" The Elders open the portals, stone pedestals appear across Norrath, and
   they announce that "the Age of Balance is nearly upon us."
   [Planes of Power](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2012/12/30/planes-of-power-2/) · B
5. **The Plane of Time.** The gods have met there to seal Zebuxoruk in a stasis nothing can break,
   and **every one of them gave part of themselves to the binding**. Weakened, the dark gods stay in
   the plane with their armies to recover. Then they feel Tallon, Vallon and Terris Thule drained and
   realise mortals are coming. The elemental powers agree to "call upon the beast we swore would never
   be needed": they fuse their powers into one towering guardian. The script only calls it "the
   beast"; **it is the creature we fight as Quarm.**
   [Plane of Time script](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2011/11/09/plane-of-time-script/) (the zone's emotes) · A
6. **The ending.** Mortals free Zebuxoruk. As he starts to share what the gods forbade, **Druzzil Ro**,
   the Matron of the Art and his old teacher, appears and forbids it, and turns time back for him and
   the mortals. He writes that he saw time split in two and does not know which copy he now lives in.
   It reads as the lore's own reason why the Plane of Time resets.
   [The Words of Zebuxoruk](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2013/06/27/the-words-of-zebuxoruk/) · A (in-game text, exact source not stated)
7. **After PoP (EverQuest 2).** The gods hold council. Rallos Zek and Solusek Ro want mortals
   destroyed. Karana points out that "any one of us alone can be overcome by the mortals". Solusek Ro
   warns that some demi-planes have already faded because the gods are spread too thin. **Quellious**
   proposes the compromise everyone signs: break mortal unity, and **withdraw the gods' influence from
   Norrath** until they recover. Rallos whispers to Cazic-Thule on the way out, and his last line
   is "Let the endgame begin." [Tome of Destiny I](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2009/12/24/chapter-i-the-council-of-the-gods/) · C
   - Then the druid rings and wizard spires stop working, Quellious sends an **Avatar of Tranquility**,
     and a new Rallosian army out of the Feerrott marches on Freeport (the Battle of Defiance).
     [The Words of the Avatar](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2013/06/27/the-words-of-the-avatar/) · C
   - Next come **the Rending**, which breaks the land and seas
     ([ch. X](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2009/12/24/chapter-x-the-rending/)), and **the Shattering**, when
     **Luclin breaks apart** and falls as meteors
     ([ch. XI](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2009/12/24/chapter-xi-the-shattering/),
     [The Wheel of Time](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2011/04/24/the-wheel-of-time/)). Survivors
     sail for new lands ([ch. XII](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2009/12/24/chapter-xii-destiny-awaits/)). · C

## From Luclin into the planes (the bridge from what we just finished)

- **Aten Ha Ra** became High Priestess of the Akheva through civil war. The Akheva of Ka Vethan say she
  sent the storm that still rages over the Maiden's Eye, as a weapon to kill their leader Akelha'Ra.
  Akelha'Ra's loyalists stayed in the ruins as outcasts.
  [Notes on the Akheva of Maiden's Eye](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2021/06/29/notes-on-the-akheva-of-maidens-eye/) · A
- A later note in the same book says Sanctus Seru scouts saw those outcasts **go over to the
  Shissar**, who promised them vengeance on Aten Ha Ra in Emperor Ssraeshza's name. (Same source.)
- **Emperor Ssraeshza** led the Shissar to Luclin to escape the Green Mist on Kunark, nearly two
  thousand years before the Combine.
  [Encyclopedia Shissar I](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2012/03/13/encyclopedia-shissar-vol-i/) · A
- **The thread to PoP:** the Elder in Tranquility names the Shissar as the race that misused
  Zebuxoruk's gift and went mad for it. So the emperor we killed ruled what was left of Zebuxoruk's
  worst failure, and the next door leads to Zebuxoruk himself. [About Zebuxoruk](https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2010/07/14/about-zebuxoruk/) · A
- **Later, Luclin herself shatters** (step 7). The moon we just finished raiding is gone in the world
  that follows PoP.

## Using it

- **Before a pull:** one line from the zone table, told as ours ("Tonight we go after the thing the
  Rathe fused themselves into…"). Keep to what the table says; the Tier D rows are "legend has it".
- **The season-long frame:** every god we kill is one the council later cites as proof mortals are
  too strong. The last kill, Quarm, ends with a goddess rewinding time rather than let us learn.
- **Surfaces this could feed** (proposals only, UI rule applies): a lore line per zone card on
  `/pop`, a story block on `/guide/[bossId]`, a flavour line on the Discord kill card, and the next
  film.

## What the agent pass got wrong (checked 2026-09-28)

So the next session does not repeat them:
- The *Tome of Destiny* chapters were filed as PoP-era setup. They are **EverQuest 2's bridge story,
  set after PoP**. The gods withdraw *because* mortals broke the planes.
- The Rending was placed after "Gates of Discord", and Mayong Mistmoore's rise was called the next
  step after PoP. Neither holds: the Rending and the Shattering are EQ2-era; the vampire
  storyline is much later EQ1 content.
- Tallon and Vallon Zek were called demigoddesses. They are male and were mortal war heroes.
- Aerin`Dar was called Mithaniel's "high priestess". It is a corrupted crystalline dragon.
- "The Veil" in the Prophecy of Trakanon essay was read as a wall between Norrath and the planes. The
  essay says it is the circle the gods of the old pact raised to keep Veeshan in check. The whole post
  is a player's essay about GM events (Tier D).
- The Cazic-Thule "Rain of Fear" story is a much later EQ1 expansion, not PoP.
- One agent padded every god with invented "raid hooks" (warming oceans, a dark forge). None of
  it is in the sources. Nothing in this doc came from those lines.
- Agnarr was guessed to be Karana's avatar. The launch story only shows him as the face that swallows
  Karana's vision, and the site says nothing more about him.
