### 547. [infra-costs] Raid Mimic is local-first: a player's data lives on their PC, the hub keeps it a week unless a guild pays, and the analytics are what is sold (direction, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead: *"pair up with mimic, main data stored locally, hosted for a week if unclaimed by a guild paying for storage or
stats. analytics are the sell not the hosting if we can use supabase files effectively. also draft the changes to privacy and
terms of service for these changes."*
- **Where data lives:** Mimic keeps a player's full record on their own PC (logs, parses, flags, gear). What reaches the hub
  for a player with no paying guild is kept **7 days**, then deleted. A guild that pays keeps its members' data for as long as
  it pays, and gets the analytics (raid reviews, merged parses, trends, attendance, flag planning).
- **How it is stored:** files in Supabase Storage, one gzipped file per fight or per upload, as the fight snapshots already are
  (§539: $0 on Pro up to 100 GB, no database disk work), with a small database index. The database holds only what pages query
  live; bulk data is files. This is what makes a free week affordable and the paid retention cheap to run.
- **What is sold:** the analytics, not the storage. Hosting is a cost line the guild's price covers, not the product.
- **Not built:** the 7-day sweep for unclaimed data, the guild claim (a guild adopting a player's hosted data), the move of hub
  data to files. The privacy and terms changes are DRAFTS (`docs/legal/DRAFT-2026-11-local-first.md`) until they ship, and
  the published pages change in the same change that makes them true (CLAUDE.md, the flag-exchange privacy rule).
