### 546. [pop-progression] raidmimic.com: skip the video, a signed-in home, and the coordination tools (direction, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead, after step 1 of §545 shipped (https://raidmimic.com/characters, web 2.1.112): *"we should have a big skip video
button at the top of raidmimic.com and also have users log into it and have their information come up on the homepage or
redirect instead of hitting the video. then have a link 'rewatch the video' up top. ... also have coordination tools handy.
(I'm looking for X/Y/Z flags/loot, when can I get aligned to someone for that, does NBG or random vs dkp/other point system.)
guild leaders can fill their ranks with classes needed. can make it so your other toons can contribute to raiding but then get
flagged on other toons. track autoraidinvite, etc"*
- **The front door (building now):** a big Skip at the top of the video; a signed-in player skips the video entirely. Two
  previews on beta for the pick: **b** "Doorway" sends a signed-in player straight to /characters, **c** "Home panel" puts their
  characters and the coordination tools at the top of the homepage. Both keep a "Rewatch the video" link at the top.
- **Coordination tools (the §545 build order, steps 2 to 4, with these added):**
  - *Looking for:* a player posts the flags or loot a character is after; raids that would give it are matched to them.
  - *Loot system as a filter:* each raid post states its system (need before greed, random rolls, DKP or another point
    system), so a player can look only at raids run the way they want.
  - *Recruiting by class:* a guild lead lists the classes the guild needs; players looking for a guild see where their class
    is wanted.
  - *Help on one toon, flagged on another:* contribution counts per PLAYER, flags land per CHARACTER, so bringing one
    character to help a raid can earn a slot to flag another. Needs the server's rules checked first: a flag NPC answers only
    the killing raid or group (`lua_modules/projection_eligibility.lua`), so the swap happens between raids, not inside one.
  - *Auto raid invite:* the raid post carries its invite character (Wolf Pack has `/autoraidinvite` today, guild-only, the
    credential officer-only); an accepted player sees "send a tell to X" on the Mimic card. The credential never leaves the
    officers.
- Asked with sample pictures as each is built, never as a bare A or B.

### 547. [infra-costs] Raid Mimic is local-first: a player's data lives on their PC, the hub keeps it a week unless a guild pays, and the analytics are what is sold (direction, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead: *"pair up with mimic, main data stored locally, hosted for a week if unclaimed by a guild paying for storage or
stats. analytics are the sell not the hosting if we can use supabase files effectively. also draft the changes to privacy and
terms of service for these changes."*
- **Where data lives:** Mimic keeps a player's full record on their own PC (logs, parses, flags, gear). What reaches the hub
  for a player with no paying guild is kept **7 days**, then deleted. A guild that pays keeps its members' data for as long as
  it pays, and gets the analytics (raid reviews, merged parses, trends, attendance, flag planning).
- **How it is stored:** files in Supabase Storage, one gzipped file per fight or per upload, as the fight snapshots already are
  (§539: $0 on Pro up to 100 GB, no database disk work), with a small database index. The database holds only what pages query
  live; bulk data is files. This is what makes a free week affordable and the paid retention cheap to run.
- **What is sold:** the analytics, not the storage. Hosting is a cost line the guild's price covers, not the product.
- **Not built:** the 7-day sweep for unclaimed data, the guild claim (a guild adopting a player's hosted data), the move of hub
  data to files. The privacy and terms changes are DRAFTS (`docs/legal/DRAFT-2026-11-local-first.md`) until they ship, and
  the published pages change in the same change that makes them true (CLAUDE.md, the flag-exchange privacy rule).

### 548. [mimic-app] A guest's data is his: how he gets it back, and what of the host's he can see (direction, picks open, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead, after guest characters reached Mimic beta (agent 4.0.81: a guest's log is read, nothing of his uploads): *"for
guest characters the machine should allow the user to feel like their data is accessible to them. optionally could we sync back
sessions from the logs to join them up? or add a streamed log back via a p2p link approved by the local user when both are
online? perhaps we can hold the guest's files separate from the main user and not display the main users files unless they
approve via a discord message?"*
- **Getting his data back** (Decision Desk, Raid Mimic hub tab, "hub-guest-data"): A take it home (one file, his Mimic or
  raidmimic.com loads it), B his own Discord sign-in on the host's Mimic for his characters only, allowed once by the host from
  Discord, uploads as him live (recommended), C a direct PC-to-PC stream. C is B in practice: home routers block direct links,
  so it falls back to a relay through our server.
- **The host's data while a guest plays** ("hub-guest-lock"): A a guest lock that hides the host's characters, tells and stats
  the moment a guest is live, unlocked only by a button in the host's own Discord DM (recommended); B a separate guest profile
  (own folder, restart each way); C as today.

### 549. [web] Players choose who sees their character links: to each other, and to their account (direction, pick open, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead: *"let people opt into and out of /who data that they themselves submit for linking characters. it would be
helpful for players to get to choose if they're publicly linked from character to character or to their accounts."*
- **Checked first:** /who data does not link characters today (`who_observations` has no owner or alt column). The links come
  from OpenDKP main/alt (`characters.main_name`), officer links (`main_name_override`), a member's own claims and mule uploads,
  and `characters.discord_id`. They show as "Alt of …" on character pages, /who, the roster, the PoP pages and Discord's /who
  and /whois. No switch hides them (`hidden_from_lists` hides a character from lists, not its links).
- **Two disclosures, two switches:** main ↔ alts, and character ↔ Discord account; each Private / Guild / Public. Default Guild,
  which is today's behaviour. Private hides a link from members, not from officers (attendance and DKP count per person).
- **Pick open** (Desk "hub-link-privacy"): A one account-wide pair, B a pair on every character, C account-wide plus
  per-character exceptions (recommended).

### 550. [mimic-overlays] "<Name> is not online": name the alt that is, and ready the tell (direction, pick open, 2026-11-09)

- **Pick open** (Desk "hub-offline-tell"): A speak it and put `/tell <alt> <message>` on the clipboard (recommended), B speak
  only, C true auto-forward once Zeal can take a command.
