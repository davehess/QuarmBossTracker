### 545. [pop-progression] raidmimic.com loads anyone's characters from the #popflags export, then schedules open flag raids (direction, 2026-11-09)
The guild lead, the night Coirnav first fell: *"now that we have pop flags coming out in export on camp, we're going to start
making raid mimic .com take in that file as a way to load in your character, as well as the other ones, and be able to treat it
similar to a quarmy. From that, we can give any of the flagging scheduling. We can set up guilds to be able to announce what
they're going to be doing, and if they are short on characters, they can fill up to 72 if they want to. Setting how many open
slots they can do, giving preference to specific other partner guilds, giving some overview on what loot will be run as for that
raid if it's flagging only or if other people will be able to roll on things. Being able to track who is who and who the raid
lead is ... have it come up on the overlay to get you in. Command center and target info are important here. This needs to happen
for anyone joining the server. For up to 8 characters. By default."*
- **What it is:** the flag exchange of `docs/DESIGN-flag-exchange.md`, now with an explicit entry point (the Zeal
  `<Name>-PoPFlags.txt` written on /camp, already read by the agent: `parsePopFlagsFile`) and an explicit audience (anyone on the
  server, not only member guilds). Scoping report of 2026-11-09: the player lane (`hub_players`, `hub_characters`, `hub_flags`,
  `utils/hubSync.js` claim rule) exists with no reader and no web import; `flag_events` is Wolf Pack-only; no open-slot, partner,
  raid-lead or loot-rule model; hub and player Mimic sessions get no poll streams (`want.clear()` in the poll handler).
- **Defaults taken from the request (no pick needed):** 8 characters per player by default, a setting; up to 72 per raid, the
  guild sets how many slots are open; partner guilds get first claim on open slots; each raid states its loot mode (flagging only,
  or open rolls) with a short note; the raid lead is named on the raid and on the overlay card.
- **Build order (proposed):** 1. the import and "my characters" on raidmimic.com (web, `hub_*` tables, the existing claim rule, cap
  8); 2. guild raid posts with open slots, partners and loot mode; 3. accept/decline and the raid-lead view; 4. the Mimic card
  (Command Center section + Target Info line) on a new poll stream scoped to raids the player was accepted to; 5. watches,
  notifications and the cross-guild privacy rewrite.
- **Still open (asked with samples when each step is built):** who accepts a guest (leader each time / first come / partners
  automatic); whether "who needs it" shows names or counts; how strong a non-member's claim to a character must be.
