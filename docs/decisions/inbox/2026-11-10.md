### 562. [infra-costs] Signer A (Azure Artifact Signing) and bot shape B (active/passive) (the guild lead, 2026-11-10)
*"go with A for signing and B for HA."* Both were the recommended options on the Decision Desk (cards `sec-code-signing`,
`bot-ha`).
- **Signing:** Azure Artifact Signing, Basic tier ($9.99/month, 5,000 signatures). The pipeline already on `main` turns on
  by itself once the six `AZURE_*` repo settings exist (`docs/code-signing.md`); the account, the identity validation and
  the settings are the guild lead's. The first signed build is a Mimic beta.
- **Bot HA:** two bots, one active and one warm standby, built in two stages. Stage 1 is a lease row in the database: only
  the holder logs in to post, runs the background jobs and the Discord side, and a deploy's new bot waits for the old one to
  let go (this alone ends the ~14 s double-bot overlap seen in the 2026-11-10 deploy). Stage 2 adds the standby, and before it
  the bot's in-memory live data (`utils/liveMemory.js`) must read from the database, because the one-replica rule exists
  only for that memory. Option C (split upload servers, Redis) waits until load needs it.
