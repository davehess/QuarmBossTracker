### 568. [infra-costs] Guests on another guild's raid: the host keeps the raid's record, never the guest's own data (the guild lead, 2026-11-12)
*"guilds like Breakfast Club that bring in non-members for raids are in an interesting position because they do need to
have information per player, but they are not in charge of those players. and the /who observations those players make if
they're not guild members would not apply to breakfast club's guild, they would stick to their local /who directory and
their guild's copy"*
- **The rule:** a guild that brings non-members on a raid keeps what happened ON that raid for each player (the fight's
  rows, attendance, loot and DKP for that raid, marked as a guest's). It does not own those players: nothing else a guest's
  own Mimic sees goes to the host. A guest's /who sightings stay on the guest's PC and go to the guest's own guild.
- **What the code does today (scoped 2026-11-12, readiness audit #20):** a guest link can already send only fights
  (`utils/mimicLink.js` allowlist), and a guest's own /who is dropped at ingest (`index.js`, the `!isGuest` check on
  `who_data`). Two gaps remain, both on the HOST side: every sighting is stored under one fixed guild id, so a host
  member's /who and the PvP harvest record non-members in the host's directory; and /anon players are stored by name with
  their earlier class and guild carried over (57,096 of 270,073 rows, 4,902 characters).
- **Settings:** `guild_settings.guests` (allow, pass length) is the guild's own switch (§569). Its pass lengths today are
  1 hour, 3 hours, tonight and 7 days (`web/lib/guestLink.ts`); the 6 hours picked on the Desk in October is not offered.
- **Open, on the Decision Desk (`guests-who`):** drop or re-home non-member sightings; honour /anon; a removal route for
  someone who never signed up; whether a guest's raid rows are purged after a time.
