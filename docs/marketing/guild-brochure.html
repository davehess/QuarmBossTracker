A raid platform for Project Quarm guilds · free to self-host

Less bookkeeping. More raiding.

A free, self-hostable platform that does the raid-night admin your officers currently do by hand — parses, boards, loot, attendance and recaps that keep themselves up to date.

One parse, not six

Every client only sees what's in range, so six people paste six different parses and someone has to referee. Here, each raider's log feeds one merged record of the fight automatically — no pasting, no screenshots, no argument. Damage and healing for every kill land in Discord and on your guild site on their own.

12,891 fights parsed and merged this way so far, across 217 recorded raid nights and 691,427,699 points of recorded damage.

The board updates itself

Boss timers live in a dedicated channel that edits in place as mobs die and windows open — nobody maintains a spreadsheet mid-raid. When the night ends, a written recap posts on its own: kills, standouts, loot, and who showed up. Attendance shows up as a simple grid instead of a tally someone keeps by hand.

135 bosses tracked, Classic through Luclin, with Planes of Power flag tracking already built and in preview ahead of the October unlock — so you can see who's flagged before anyone has to ask.

Your raiders see it too, not just your officers

A desktop overlay puts DPS and heals, trigger warnings with countdowns, buff and charm/pet status, mob info and who needs a rebuff right on screen — no alt-tabbing mid-fight. New members get walked through setup (Zeal install, the Windows Defender exception, log import) instead of being handed a wiki page, and quiet mode does exactly what it says: mute the voice callouts, nothing else.

13 overlays ship with the client, plus 88 slash commands covering raid, loot and roster management.

Loot, and everything else, stays yours

Loot bids stay sealed and encrypted until reveal, and DKP balances and wishlists are one command away instead of a spreadsheet nobody remembers to update. Officer chat never leaves the raider's machine, and tells are filtered out too by default — the private tell-history relay is opt-in and off unless a raider turns it on for themselves. Custom EverQuest UI layouts can be backed up to the cloud in one click, so a crash doesn't cost someone an evening rebuilding theirs.

Sealed bids are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) until reveal; log filtering happens locally, before upload — see docs/PRIVACY.md.

Run it yourself, free

Clone the repo and follow docs/SELFHOSTING.md — budget an evening or two for first setup. Your own box costs nothing but electricity; hosting it yourself on cloud services runs about $30/mo. Free to use, modify and self-host for your own guild.

Don't want to run it at all?

Ask us about a managed setup in whichever size fits — a starter bot with overlays, or the full guild site on your own domain. Plain-language draft terms are in docs/TERMS-hosted.md — no uptime guarantee or service-level promise, and you own your data and your domain. Open an issue titled Request from a guild to start.

Free to use, modify and self-host for your own guild. Offering it to other guilds for money needs a conversation with us first — and every release automatically becomes fully open-source (AGPL) four years after it ships, so the community is never locked out.
