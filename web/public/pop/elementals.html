The plan, at a chosen damage rate

Coirnav can't be hit at first. Killing his Guardian starts a 15-minute clock. Three waves of 25 fiends arrive at 0:00, 3:00 and 5:00 whether or not the last wave is dead. When all 75 are dead, Coirnav can be attacked and three stronger minibosses appear beside him. The raid's plan: skip the minibosses and kill Coirnav. The script counts that as a win, the minibosses vanish with him, and you avoid what killing them sets off (his debuffs wiped and 25 lings every 49 seconds). Miss 15:00 and Coirnav banishes everyone from the zone. You can try again 20 minutes later, and a failed attempt does not use up his 138-hour lockout.

5,900

What actually fails this event is not damage. At our median we finish with about eight minutes spare. It fails on these instead: (1) a tracked fiend or miniboss that disappears without a confirmed kill for 10 seconds ends the event, so no evacuating, no zoning a mob out, and no pulling them out of the reef; (2) the three early minibosses (2 million hit points, rampage) have to be held by off-tanks for five to six minutes while the fiends die; (3) if the raid does kill the minibosses, 25 lings rise every 49 seconds until Coirnav is dead: don't stop to clean them up.

The reef, from above

North is up. The zone is a deep bowl: you arrive at the surface in the south, and Coirnav waits about 500 feet down in the far north-east. Colour is depth. Tap a marker for what it is and how to deal with it.

The four named of the reef

Not needed for the flag. Pull each one to the entrance (the zone-in) and fight it there: short swim, the exit right there, nothing else close. Each respawns 18 hours after it dies, sees invisible and summons. All of them, and Coirnav, are immune to cold (cold resist 1,000): wizards and druids bring fire and magic, not ice. Times are at our median damage rate.

Getting past the trash

  • Invisibility versus undead is useless here: every mob in the zone sees through it. Use normal invisibility.
  • Most plain fish and shell trash can't see invisible (the sharks, barracuda, swordfish, turtles, kraken, anglerfish, the Hraquis on the road to Coirnav, most Triloun and Regrua). An invisible raid swims past them.
  • These see invisible: Triloun champions (upper reef, west), Regrua sentries (lower reef, centre), the ancient Regrua sentinel (lower reef, 120k hit points), the Guardian of Coirnav, every named and every rare roamer. They're red on the map.
  • Aggro range is short (35) on almost everything. The exception is right below the zone-in: the Triloun water guardians aggro from 75. Swim wide of them on the way down.
  • Swarm traps: 46 spots across the reef. One in ten is a live swarm (sees invisible, can't be hurt). Touch one in combat and 2 to 4 level 61 young fish (11,800 hit points each) spawn on you. Watch your area spells.
  • Trash respawns every 20.5 minutes. A path cleared at the start is back before a long night ends.
  • No binding in this zone, so a death sends you home. The only way out is the sponge by the zone-in, back to the Plane of Tranquility.
  • Enchanter pets: large swordfish. Level 64, 27,500 hit points, hits up to 590 at a fast 14 delay, and they can be charmed. Reef swordfish (63) work too. Not the greater swordfish: it can't be charmed.
  • Partly slowable is everywhere down here. Many mobs shrug off part of a slow and the game doesn't tell you: the kraken, anglerfish, Regrua and Hraquis lose half of it, the rare roamers 50 to 99%. The "Slow resisted" column says how much.
  • Levitate works here. Our data has nothing on breath: carry Enduring Breath or a breath item until someone confirms you don't need it.

Flag and loot

  • When Coirnav dies, the Essence of Water rises at his corpse and stays 20 minutes.
  • Hail it for the Sphere of Coalesced Water, one of the four pieces toward the Plane of Time. You must have been in the raid (or group) that killed him, and in the zone. Alts that join that raid late still count.
  • It hands out at most 72, and nothing to anyone already holding the Sphere or the Quintessence of the Elements.
  • Coirnav drops the Hammer of Crushing Waves, Mail of Storms, Sharkbone Plated Shoulderpads, the Kelp-lined Mantle, the Earring of Embedded Coral, the Double Woven Kelp Cord and elemental armour patterns.
  • Optional: the Fishlords (Lezom and Craiyk, middle reef) summon anglerfish for 100 platinum: a farming event with no flag, not worth raid time.

Our time here so far

Coirnav cleared on 8 November 2026 (the gold split at the top of this tab). Before that night: 99 pulls since Oct 21, all small groups of 1 to 10. The useful part is speed: swimming invisible we covered about 5 units a second, zone-in to the first real pull took 1 to 14 minutes, and to a named kill about 11.
