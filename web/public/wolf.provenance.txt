wolf.png — provenance
=====================
Generated by Google Gemini (Flash Extended) on 2026-08-28, from a prompt
authored in this repo for the landing-page redesign. The prompt specified a
frontal, bilaterally symmetric wolf head built from filled tapering forms, one
flat colour, no background, sized to survive both a 600px hero and a 40%-scale
blurred copy behind it.

Gemini returned bone artwork on a near-black ground rather than transparency —
the documented fallback in that prompt. Post-processing in this repo, no
external service: luminance keyed to alpha (45→205 ramp), all pixels flattened
to the bone token #E8E2D4, the generator's corner sparkle watermark cut, the
artwork auto-cropped to its bounding box and re-squared with an even margin.
Source 1024×1024 → shipped 973×973.

Not traced from, and not derived from, any third-party illustration. The
reference image discussed during the session was used to describe a STYLE in
words; no part of it was copied into this asset.

wolf-eyes.png — derived, same session
=====================================
Not generated and not drawn: computed from wolf.png in this repo. The keyed
plate leaves the eye interior as two small OPAQUE islands, isolated from the
face by the dark linework that keying turned transparent. Those two islands
were found by connected-component labelling of the alpha channel, kept with
their own alpha (so the edge stays antialiased) and painted flat #FFCF5C on the
same 973x973 canvas. The pupil is a hole in each island and stays a hole.

Same canvas is deliberate: the glow carries no coordinates, so it is pinned
edge-to-edge over the wolf and cannot drift from it. Regenerating wolf.png at
any other size means regenerating this one too — test/wolf-eyeglow.test.js
fails if the two ever disagree.
