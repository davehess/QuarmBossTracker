zeal/marks/*.png: provenance
============================
Rendered in this repo, with no external service, from the real Zeal tag meshes: the tag-shapes
branch of the guild lead's Zeal fork (tag_shapes.cpp), dumped by
docs/upstream/zeal-tag-shapes/preview/dump.cpp and rasterised by export_marks.py in the same folder.
Colours come from nameplate.cpp's TagArrowColor values and the kGuilds table. Each file is a
face-on view, 160x160 with a transparent background.

- icon-<name>.png: the symbol keys (K X A D F T WP M U N H $ E).
- badge-<n>.png: the numbered badges ^1^ to ^12^.
- paw-<c>.png: the paw with a letter or digit, ^P<c>^.
- banner-<CODE>.png and guild-<CODE>.png: each guild's flag (banner) ^F<CODE>^ and icon ^I<CODE>^.

The guild icons are our own drawings, made for each guild's name or theme. No guild's artwork
was traced for them.

To regenerate after a shape changes, from a checkout of the fork:
  g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -I<zeal>/Zeal -o dump dump.cpp <zeal>/Zeal/tag_shapes.cpp && ./dump > meshes.json
  python3 export_marks.py meshes.json <zeal>/Zeal/tag_shapes.cpp <zeal>/Zeal/tag_arrows.cpp <web>/public/zeal/marks

zeal/tagicons/*: provenance
===========================
Picture files for Zeal's tag pictures (^I<name>^ with <name>.png or .tga in uifiles/zeal/tagicons).

- EUR.png, EUR.tga: Europa's guild logo, the shield with "EUROPA" across it. Their guild leader
  first sent a logo on 2026-09-25 and asked for it to be made into a Zeal tag icon; the guild lead
  supplied this version on 2026-10-09 ("Try making this the Europa logo"). Processed in this repo:
  - the dark background was removed by flood fill from the edges, after closing the shield's
    outline (11 px) so its dark band and rim stay in; background-coloured shadow within 11 px of
    the outside was then dropped and the edge feathered by about a pixel;
  - it was cropped to the shield and scaled to 226x256, then (2026-10-10) to 113x128, because the
    fork's tag pictures refuse anything over 128 pixels a side (kMaxImagePixels in tag_arrows.cpp,
    lowered from 512 in the fork's review fixes) and the 226x256 file would have been skipped in game.
  The TGA is the same pixels as the PNG, 32-bit with alpha. The logo belongs to Europa.

Every picture file here must pass Zeal's own checks: a PNG or true-colour TGA, at most 128 pixels a
side, at most 1 MB. test/zeal-icons-page.test.js enforces this (PICTURE_RULES in web/lib/zealIcons.ts).
